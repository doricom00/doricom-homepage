CEO Message

Every installation should earn lifelong trust,
down to the parts you never see.

Hello, I am Seungah Shin, CEO of DORICOM Co., Ltd.
Thank you for visiting our website.

Telecommunications have become the foundation of every business. When a single line or cable fails, office work, hospital care and store sales all stop with it. At DORICOM, we take pride in making sure these essentials always simply work.

Built around ICT construction, DORICOM handles everything from network installation and maintenance to CCTV and security, internet, phone, IPTV and store solutions. Instead of dealing with several vendors, our clients work with one team from consultation and design through installation and after-service.

Every industry has different needs. Hospitals need uninterrupted stability, stores need fast installation around business hours, and companies and public institutions need systematic management. Through the KNN Future Industry Leaders Forum and the Advanced Healthcare Management Program at Pusan National University School of Medicine, I continue to learn what each industry truly needs, because we believe the right network starts with understanding our clients' work.

Careful, thorough workmanshipWe work meticulously from the start, so after-service is rarely needed.

Our engineers, with over 25 years in the field, install every cable to standard, even inside the ceiling. For us, good work means preventing problems rather than fixing them later.

After installation, we stay close with fast response and friendly service. Our goal is to turn every project into a long-term partnership.

DORICOM will keep growing as an ICT specialist that understands each client's industry and can be trusted with their infrastructure. Thank you for your continued support.

Thank you.

CEO, DORICOM Co., Ltd. Seungah Shin
